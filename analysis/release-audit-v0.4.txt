Scientific scope
The supported contribution is artifact-specific empirical evidence: a missing
relation in a pinned Circom benchmark and an authenticated-output mismatch in an
archived Nova consumer. The latter also survives an extracted client-block test.

audit principles already exist. The paper attributes them rather than claiming
