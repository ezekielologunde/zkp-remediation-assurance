EMPIRICAL CONTRIBUTION GATE, OCTOBER 14, 2026

Purpose
Assess the contribution actually supported by the artifacts, rather than require
an unrelated new algorithm or treat a known vulnerability class as a new class.
This assessment is exploratory and follows the original findings; it is not a
preregistered discovery protocol.

Candidate claim
Two pinned research execution paths fail specific connections required by the
associated design: a signed-accumulator connection in a Circom benchmark and
an authenticated-output-to-expected-root comparison in a Nova consumer. Their
different failure locations require different diagnostic controls. A shared
successful-proof symptom does not imply a common patch.

Closest-work matrix
Tang et al. 2024, Zero-Knowledge Proof Vulnerability Analysis and Security Auditing
https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/514.pdf, section 4.2.
Contribution: layered auditing including application design and integration.
Overlap: the general audit principle. It invalidates a new-method claim here.
Difference: the present artifact observations and recorded paired controls are
the empirical object, not an invented auditing taxonomy.

Ootani 2026, Context-Binding Gaps in Stateful Zero-Knowledge Proximity Proofs
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.03900v1, sections III-F, VII-F and VIII-B.
Contribution: compares binding strategies and operational assumptions in a
geo-content system, including correctly enforced server-side alternatives.
Overlap: proof validity versus application context and diagnostic comparisons.
Difference: our study executes third-party attestation/SBOM paths. It does not
claim the general separation or in-proof binding as new. The full text scopes
its empirical results to geo-content, not our two named artifact discrepancies.

zkFuzz 2026, https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~junfeng/papers/zkfuzz/
Contribution: execution-trace/constraint consistency testing of ZK circuits.
Overlap: circuit mutation and real-proof controls are established techniques.
Difference: our accepted mutation satisfies both witness code and constraints;
the missing link is the specified application relation. Our Rust case is a
consumer output selection issue. No measured comparison to zkFuzz was run, so
do not claim detector superiority or a demonstrated tool false negative.

PIRANHAS 2026, https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-f526-paper.pdf
Contribution: anonymous single-device and swarm attestation and performance.
Obligation: signed accumulator and key relation; pinned README C1/E1 identifies
the Groth16 benchmark. Our result is a benchmark correspondence discrepancy,
not an invalidation of its theorem or an exploitation of its full protocol.

VeriSBOM 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13682v1
Contribution: private SBOM verification against authoritative roots.
Obligation: section5.3 and Algorithm1 require root matching at verification.
Our mismatch changes auxiliary metadata while retaining the authenticated proof.
It is not the already acknowledged issue of stale trusted roots or incomplete
SBOM input. An honest committed SBOM in a synthetic fixture is sufficient for
the tested consumer mismatch.

Decision
Supported: a distinct, bounded empirical account relative to the reviewed work.
Unsupported: a novel cryptographic primitive, novel audit principle, first-ever
discovery, representative prevalence, analyzer superiority, deployed exploit.
Journal suitability remains an editorial judgment. Scientific submission should
use the narrow empirical claim and preserve all scope limitations. A complete
draft is not journal acceptance and does not remove the author's review duty.
